## How I design systems

```text
DISCOVER
   ↓
OBSERVE / COLLECT EVIDENCE
   ↓
NORMALIZE
   ↓
ANALYZE
   ↓
PLAN
   ↓
VALIDATE
   ↓
APPROVAL GATE
   ↓
EXECUTE
   ↓
VERIFY
   ↓
AUDIT / ROLLBACK
```

The principles behind that workflow:

- **Security by design** — authority is explicit, bounded and reviewable.
- **Evidence before assumption** — important conclusions should be traceable to observable facts.
- **Human-in-the-loop for critical actions** — automation should not silently cross sensitive boundaries.
- **Verification after change** — a command succeeding is not the same as the system being correct.
- **Rollback is part of deployment** — recovery is designed before mutation, not after failure.
- **Local-first when confidentiality matters** — keep sensitive workloads and evidence under operator control.
- **Observability first** — systems should explain what they are doing and why.

## Current direction

My current engineering priority is to turn **networking, cybersecurity, monitoring and diagnostics** into a coherent operational platform before expanding the AI layer further.

The target is not simply another dashboard. The system should support a disciplined operational loop:

> **Observe → Diagnose → Collect Evidence → Request Authority → Act → Verify → Audit**

AI belongs inside that loop as an analyst and advisor, while deterministic policy and explicit permissions remain the authority boundary.
